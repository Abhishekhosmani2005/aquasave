🌿 Practical ways to save water

Choose habits that fit your household. Actual savings vary with usage and local conditions.

🚿

Shorten showers

Reduce shower time and turn water off while soaping up.

🔧

Fix leaks promptly

Check taps, toilet cisterns and exposed pipes regularly.

🪥

Turn off the tap

Do not leave water running while brushing or scrubbing.

🪣

Use a bucket

Where practical, use a bucket instead of a running hose.

🌱

Reuse suitable water

Reuse safe, appropriate household water for plants when possible.

🚰

Prevent tank overflow

Check storage tanks and arrange a timely shutoff or repair.
